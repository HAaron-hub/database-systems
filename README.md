# database-systems
Database Systems 数据库系统

## Exams and Grading Policy / 考试与成绩评定

- Attendance / 出勤: 20%
- Homework (2 assigns) / 作业（2次）: 30%
- Final Examination / 期末考试: 50%

**Warning / 注意：**

Attendance rate below 50% will result in unconditional failure of the course.
Attendance taking begins at the 4th week and covers 13 sessions. The minimum requirement is to attend (have attendance recorded for) at least 7 out of the 13 sessions. Do NOT sign for others; methods will be used to detect fraud.

出勤率低于50%将无条件不及格。
出勤记录从第4周开始，共计13次课。最低要求是在这13次出勤记录中至少获取7次出勤。请勿代签，课程将采用手段检测和识别代签行为。
